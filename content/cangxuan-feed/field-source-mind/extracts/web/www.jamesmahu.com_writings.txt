The writings of James Mahu span a broad range of formats, to include: novels, spiritual essays, compilations, poetry, websites, and interviews. The consistent core of his writings revolve around the personal mythology he came to call WingMakers. The writings are organized below with links to each section.
His writings have been translated into 18 languages and widely read across the globe. Perhaps the best way to understand his writings are to study
The Collected Works of the WingMakers Vol. 1
or to read his novel
Quantusum
. While each of those require significant investments in time, you might also try the Blog for shorter renderings of James’ writing.
