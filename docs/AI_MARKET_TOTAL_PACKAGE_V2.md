## Acceptance protocol
1. AI isolated Node tests pass on the PR head.
2. Site-wide CI passes without waivers; existing SASI and /tools failures must be fixed by their maintainers.
3. Verified data rights and source agreements are recorded.
4. Durable ingestion, recovery, stale-data alerts and per-model history tested with real observations.
5. Staging browsers verify search, sort, compare, export, locale, accessibility and error fallback.
6. Production preview remains disabled until release audit has zero blockers.

No fabricated popularity, usage or pricing metrics. Unknown is not zero. Paid placement must never alter organic ranks. Do not delete /tools or SASI.
