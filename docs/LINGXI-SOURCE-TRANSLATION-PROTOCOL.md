# Lingxi Source Translation Protocol

Version: 1.0
Status: mandatory for all paid-product knowledge nodes

## Purpose

The private corpus is a foundation for thinking, not a phrase library. Lingxi Field absorbs underlying mechanisms and rewrites them into original, observable, non-deterministic knowledge. Reports must never depend on a reader knowing the source world or accepting its metaphysical claims.

## Eight Mechanism Axes

1. **Inner coherence**: whether values, decisions, language, and repeated actions support or contradict one another.
2. **Choice ownership**: whether a person can distinguish a chosen commitment from an inherited expectation or automatic reaction.
3. **Relational emergence**: what becomes possible only through interaction, rather than being attributed entirely to either individual.
4. **Contradiction integration**: whether opposing needs can be held long enough to form a third workable option.
5. **Embodied verification**: whether an insight can be checked in behavior, memory, sensation, schedule, exchange, or relationship.
6. **Attention direction**: what repeatedly receives observation and therefore gains decision weight.
7. **Boundary and reciprocity**: how giving, receiving, dependence, trust, and refusal remain sustainable.
8. **Service orientation**: whether a recommendation preserves agency, reduces fear, and benefits more than the immediate transaction.

These are editorial axes, not scientific diagnoses or claims about hidden physical forces.

## Required Translation Chain

Every source-informed idea must pass through this sequence:

```text
private source fingerprint
→ neutral mechanism
→ observable behavior
→ conditions that strengthen or weaken it
→ counterexample that can disprove the reading
→ bounded action or reflection
→ safety review
→ original Lingxi wording
→ product/chapter/node placement
```

A node is rejected if it cannot answer all four questions:

- What is the mechanism?
- What real evidence could the user recognize?
- When would this interpretation not apply?
- What can the user do without surrendering agency?

## Forbidden Reuse

- Do not use original document titles as report headings, products, chapters, practices, or marketing language.
- Do not use named fictional systems or lore-specific identity labels.
- Do not copy sentences, distinctive metaphors, taxonomies, or extended wording.
- Do not present spiritual or fictional concepts as established neuroscience, physics, medicine, or psychology.
- Do not replace evidence with authority phrases such as “the source says” or “research proves” without a real, reviewable citation.
- Do not issue medical diagnoses, financial instructions, destiny claims, or dependency-inducing guidance.

The global knowledge lint blocks expressly excluded terms. Editorial review remains responsible for semantic imitation that exact-term checks cannot detect.

## Global Pain-Point Research Gate

Before expanding one of the ten paid products, create a dated demand brief from current global evidence.

Required evidence layers:

1. Search behavior: current queries, question patterns, and language differences by market.
2. User voice: recurring problems and desired outcomes from public communities, reviews, and support conversations.
3. Authoritative context: primary research, official guidance, and applicable regulatory or safety material.
4. Product gap: what existing products summarize, overpromise, omit, or make difficult to act on.
5. Constructive demand: a need Lingxi should cultivate because it increases agency, reflection, relational responsibility, or practical follow-through.

Research does not become report prose. It determines coverage priorities, scenarios, counterevidence, and action protocols.

## Provenance Record

Each reviewed concept should retain:

- `conceptId`: original Lingxi mechanism identifier.
- `sourceHashes`: private SHA-256 prefixes only; never public filenames.
- `products` and `chapters`: intended coverage.
- `mechanism`: neutral explanation.
- `observableEvidence`: user-checkable evidence.
- `counterevidence`: conditions that invalidate the reading.
- `safetyClass`: normal, sensitive, or prohibited.
- `reviewedBy`, `reviewedAt`, and `knowledgeVersion`.

Runtime reports expose activated Lingxi node IDs and knowledge versions, not private source identities.

## Release Gate

A product can be called deterministic and zero-token only when:

- every chapter and required semantic band has reviewed nodes;
- all default and important cross structures render without gaps;
- Chinese and English content are independently reviewed;
- deterministic fixtures pass repeatedly;
- forbidden-term, fatalism, vague-advice, citation-marker, and safety audits pass;
- the route contains no model endpoint, model credential access, or network prose generation;
- web and PDF render the same report document.
