# Daily Tide Product Research Brief

Date: 2026-08-11
Status: product input, not report prose

## User pain translated into product rules

1. Vague daily readings create temporary recognition but no durable value. Every chapter therefore includes calculated evidence, a falsification question, and an action that can be checked later.
2. People under uncertainty can seek more certainty until action stalls. The report normalizes uncertainty and returns decision authority to the user instead of promising prediction.
3. Advice is often too broad to execute. The practice chapter uses a cue-linked if-then protocol and a reversible ten-minute action.
4. A single future number hides the path. The 7, 30, and 90-day chapters use daily sampled ranges, averages, rising/falling days, and turning-point counts.
5. Lunar alignment is often overstated as local tidal or personal causation. Lingxi explicitly separates reproducible astronomy, an alignment index, and interpretive reflection.

## Evidence used for design

- Wang et al. (2021), meta-analysis of mental contrasting with implementation intentions:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34054628/
- Compernolle et al. (2019), self-monitoring interventions meta-analysis:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31409357/
- Birrell et al. (2011), uncertainty seeking and action paralysis:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21871853/
- Akbari et al. (2026), preregistered meta-analytic model of intolerance of uncertainty, worry, and anxiety:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42419154/

These sources support action design and uncertainty safeguards. They do not validate astrology or prove that astronomical configurations cause personal outcomes.
