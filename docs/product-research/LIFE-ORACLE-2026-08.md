# Life Oracle Research Brief — 2026-08

## Product problem

Symbolic self-knowledge products lose value when they rely on flattering, high-base-rate statements that feel personal but cannot be tested. The Life Oracle therefore treats each sign as a prompt for structured observation, not as proof of personality or a prediction.

## Product requirements derived from evidence

- Every chapter includes exact sign IDs and life-vector scores.
- Every interpretation includes a counterexample condition.
- Users test a reading against three recent behaviors rather than accepting resonance as proof.
- Practice recommendations use an if-then implementation intention tied to a specific trigger.
- Ability scores preserve the original 0-100 structure and are not lifted by an artificial floor.
- Symbolic stages are review coordinates with completion metrics, not forecasts.

## Evidence used for product design

- Barnum-effect research shows that people can accept broad or bogus personality feedback as self-descriptive; this supports requiring discriminating evidence and counterexamples.
- Implementation-intention research supports converting broad goals into specific trigger-action plans.
- These sources inform safety and interaction design only. Their language is not copied into paid reports.
