# Life Mirror Product Research Brief

Date: 2026-08-11
Status: product input, not report prose

## User pain translated into product rules

1. Symbolic readings are vulnerable to selective recognition and Barnum-style acceptance. Every chapter asks for mismatch and counterexamples.
2. Future-oriented cards can transfer decision authority away from the user. "Future" is defined only as a testable possibility direction, never an event forecast.
3. Emotional prose can feel valuable without changing behavior. Every chapter includes a reversible action and stop condition.
4. Reflection can become rumination. The report separates facts, interpretation, alternative interpretation, and an external test.
5. Relationship cards can encourage mind-reading. The engine explicitly prioritizes consent, communication, sustained behavior, and boundaries.
