# Lingxi Paid Report Content Audit - 2026-08-12

## Decision

The ten paid reports have a credible deterministic chapter architecture, but the relationship knowledge graph is not yet at a premium coverage level. Length alone must not be used as the quality proxy.

## Evidence

- Supplied exports range from 13 to 24 image-based PDF pages and roughly 10 MB to 19 MB.
- Core generators already use judgment, evidence, mechanism, scenario, shadow, counter-evidence, and action slots.
- Relationship `nodes.json` and `combos.json` currently duplicate the same four authored cross-nodes.
- Life Map has the strongest evidence-chain structure. Relationship products have the largest gap between page count and authored cross-node depth.

## Expansion Priority

1. Trust repair: ambiguity, secrecy, consistency, evidence of reliability, and repair after rupture.
2. Boundary negotiation: closeness versus autonomy, family intrusion, digital contact, time, and privacy.
3. Conflict protocols: escalation signals, pause rules, re-entry language, apology quality, and repeat-pattern thresholds.
4. Business authority: decision rights, veto conditions, role clarity, deadlock resolution, and exit clauses.
5. Money and exchange: risk limits, contribution visibility, unequal investment, debt, pricing, and profit allocation.
6. Relationship lifecycle: first contact, commitment, pressure, transition, repair, and conscious ending.

Each new node must contain a trigger, evidence, mechanism, real-life scene, shadow risk, counter-evidence question, a seven-day protocol, and a safe-language constraint. Memorable value should come from specificity and agency, never fear, dependency, or destiny claims.
