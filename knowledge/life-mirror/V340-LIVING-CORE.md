# life-mirror · V340 Living Knowledge Core

This product owns an independent eleven-chapter question system in `lib/report-v340/product-chapter-registry.ts` and an independent evidence-to-node projection in the V340 compiler.

Invariants:

- Never fall back to `knowledge/resilience` or another product library.
- A chapter requires at least two independent evidence contexts.
- Strength, cost, lived scene, and falsifier travel together.
- Missing evidence remains unasserted; it is never filled by generic prose.
